# RainLock Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 21, 2026
_Last updated: March 21, 2026_
**Version:** 1.0.0

RainLock ("we," "our," or "us") is a security and lock screen app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Information We Collect

**RainLock does not collect any personal data. All data stays on your device.**

### Data Stored Locally
- Lock screen configurations, security rules, biometric preferences, NFC tag registrations, and location-based unlock zones are stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local storage.
- No account or sign-in is required.

## How We Use Your Information

- Apply your configured lock and unlock rules (biometric, NFC, location-based).
- Display lock screen according to your customisations.

We do not transmit your security configuration to any SudarshanTechLabs server. We do not sell or share your data.

## Storage and Retention

- All configuration data is stored in encrypted local storage on your device.
- Data is retained until you reset settings or uninstall the app.

## Data Security

- All security settings are stored in encrypted local storage.
- Android Keystore is used for biometric-related credential storage where applicable.
- No data is ever transmitted over a network.

## Permissions Used

- **USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT**: Required to unlock the device using fingerprint or face authentication.
- **NFC**: Required to read NFC tags registered as unlock triggers.
- **ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION**: Used to enable location-aware unlock zones (e.g. unlock at home). Location is processed locally and never transmitted.
- **RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED**: Required to re-activate the lock screen service after device restart.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE**: Required to keep the lock screen service running reliably in the background.

## Your Rights and Controls

- Reset all security settings at any time from within the app.
- Revoke any permission in Android Settings → Apps → RainLock → Permissions.
- Uninstall the app to remove all configurations.

## Children's Privacy

RainLock is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
